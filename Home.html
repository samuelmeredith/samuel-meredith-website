— How it works

From first message to live website.

Here's exactly what working together looks like, from first message to a live website you're proud of.

— 01

Get in touch

Fill in the contact form or send an email. I'll come back to you within one business day for a quick chat about what you need.

— 02

Discovery brief

I'll send through a short form to get a full picture of your business: what you do, who your customers are, and what you need the site to achieve.

— 03

Send your content

You'll get a link to upload your photos, logos, and any existing copy. Not a writer? I can take care of the copy for you.

Copy add-on available
— 04

Choose a direction

I'll put together a wireframe PDF with three design options — different layouts, different feels. You pick the one that fits, and that becomes the foundation for the build.

— 05

Build and review

I'll build out the pages and send you a live draft link to click through. Review it, request changes, and sign off when you're happy.

— 06

Go live

Once you're happy, I'll connect the site to your domain. Need one? I'll point you in the right direction and walk you through the setup.

— 07

Get found

Full SEO analysis and fixes, Google Analytics setup, and Google Business Profile so your new site shows up when people search for you.

Optional add-on
— 08

Ongoing support

Need something updated as your business grows? Care plans and one-off edits are available to keep the site current.
