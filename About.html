— About · 04 / 05 Central Coast, NSW

Hey, I'm Samuel.

Years in, the part that still gets me is when a client sees their new site for the first time and it actually looks like them. When it feels right, works well, and they can't wait to share it.

I'm based on the Central Coast and I work mostly with local businesses, tradies, and personal brands who need a quality website without the agency price tag or the runaround.

When you work with me, you're not dealing with a big team or an account manager. It's just me, which means you'll always know what's happening, timelines are realistic, and I stand behind what I deliver.

I build with WordPress, Shopify, and custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript depending on what the project actually needs, not just whatever's easiest. Fast load times, proper mobile layouts, and sites Google can actually find — because a good-looking site that nobody sees isn't doing its job.

Every site I build is custom, designed from scratch to actually look the part. The process is straightforward, the pricing is fair, and I'm with you the whole way through.
