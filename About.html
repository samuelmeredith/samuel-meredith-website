— About · 04 / 05 Central Coast, NSW

Hey, I'm Samuel.

Samuel — at home, Central Coast

I started building websites because I love the moment when someone sees their new site for the first time and it actually looks like them — when it feels right, works well, and makes them proud to hand out their URL.

I'm based on the Central Coast and I work mostly with local businesses, tradies, and personal brands who need a quality website without the agency price tag or the runaround.

When you work with me, you're not dealing with a big team or an account manager. It's just me, which means you'll always know what's happening, timelines are realistic, and I stand behind what I deliver.

I build with WordPress, Shopify, and custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript depending on what the project actually needs, not just whatever's easiest. Fast load times, proper mobile layouts, and sites Google can actually find — because a good-looking site that nobody sees isn't doing its job.

Every site I build is custom, designed from scratch to actually look the part. The process is straightforward, the pricing is fair, and I'm with you the whole way through.

— How I work

Four things I won't
budge on.

The principles I keep coming back to — quietly, on every project, whether the client notices or not.

  • — 01 / Honest

    I'll tell you what you actually need, not upsell you on things you don't. If a one-page site is the right call, I'll say so — even if a five-pager would pay me more.

  • — 02 / Clear

    No confusing jargon. You'll always know where your project is at — what's next, what I need from you, and when it'll be done.

  • — 03 / Local

    I'm right here on the Central Coast. A real person you can actually talk to, meet for a coffee, and call back if something needs fixing.

  • — 04 / Affordable

    Quality work doesn't have to cost a fortune. I keep my prices fair, my scope realistic, and my add-ons honest — no surprise invoices.
